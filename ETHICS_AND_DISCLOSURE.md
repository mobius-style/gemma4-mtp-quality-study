model artifacts, synthetic performance prompts, and machine-generated outputs.

crowd-worker judgments. No human or LLM judge is used for the primary quality
endpoint.

systems are not authors and cannot accept scholarly accountability. The human

accepted for publication, interpretation, citations, claims, final text, venue
